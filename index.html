A one-day public festival celebrating the energy and diversity of contemporary African creativity — music, fashion, film, games, digital art and creative technology under one roof.

Expect live performances, DJs, a fashion show, screenings, interactive installations, panel discussions, creative workshops and a vibrant marketplace. A collaboration between Creatives Garage (Kenya) and B-Side Festival (UK), celebrating Nairobi’s evolving creative scene.
