Case Study · 2024

E-SAFIRI

Documenting Impact — a visual record of community-led climate action in Kisumu, Kenya.

Client The Earthshot Prize Location Kisumu, Kenya Role Director / Photographer
The Project

Documenting Impact

As an artist and an environmentalist, it is always a full-circle moment to be handpicked for an assignment that is not only captivating but equally tied to a social cause.

This project documents E-Safiri, a nominee of The Earthshot Prize, whose work is centred on reducing urban carbon footprint while empowering local communities. Filmed on location in Kisumu, it captures people-driven climate solutions, resilience at the grassroots, and the tangible impact of sustainable innovation within the community.

E-Safiri is a community-driven initiative based in Kisumu, focused on reducing the city’s carbon footprint while creating sustainable solutions for everyday living.

Through clean mobility, environmental awareness and local empowerment, E-Safiri works closely with communities to promote climate action that is practical, inclusive and impactful. Their work centres on people — supporting livelihoods, encouraging responsible choices, and building a cleaner, more sustainable future from the ground up.

As an environmentalist, this story spoke to me. It reminded me why I do what I do: using my lens to tell real stories, about real people, creating real change.
